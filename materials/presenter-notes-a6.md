## Preflight

- Test both SVG zooms; the crawl-walk-run strip is the closing slide - check it reads from the back row.
- Know the org's actual AI initiatives if presenting internally; Part 1 gets personal fast.
- Have the sibling-course links ready to paste in chat at minute 41.

## Never cut

- Garbage warehouse = confidently wrong AI (the session in one line).
- The freshness filter question - it saves real money within a quarter.
- Room self-placement on crawl-walk-run.
- The embarrassment question: "if we pointed an AI agent at the warehouse tomorrow, what would embarrass us?"

## Cut if long

- The views/materialized-views card (one sentence + pointer to b7).
- Two of the three vendor claims (keep zero-ETL skepticism).
- The where-next card - compress to naming the three doors.

## Likely exec questions

- "Should we pause AI until the warehouse is perfect?" - "No - sequence it. Pilot AI on your ONE well-governed mart while walk-stage work widens the base."
- "Everyone says real-time. Are we behind?" - "Behind on what decision? If nothing is acted on in minutes, daily batch is a strength, not a gap."
- "Is our warehouse AI-ready?" - "Ask the embarrassment question in your next review - the first three answers are the readiness report."
- "Warehouse or lakehouse for AI?" - "Either serves AI well if governed. The discipline transfers; the label matters less every year - a4's estates logic still applies."
