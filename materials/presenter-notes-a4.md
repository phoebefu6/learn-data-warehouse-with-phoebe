# Presenter notes · Leader session a4 · The buy landscape

## Run of show (45 min)

| Minutes | Beat |
|---------|------|
| 0-3 | Welcome. Frame: "a vendor deck is on someone's desk right now. Today you learn to read it." |
| 3-11 | Part 1 estates SVG: warehouse = finished office, lake = cheap land, lakehouse = newest build. Land the amber note: warehouse first. |
| 11-15 | Lakehouse-bought-too-early story. Ask the room: "who has seen a tool bought for the demo?" |
| 15-20 | One-sentence definitions drill: have 2-3 execs say each estate back in their own words. |
| 20-29 | Part 2 engine table, row by row. Spend the time on pricing shape + watch out, skip feature talk. Say the health warning out loud: re-verify pricing the week you buy. |
| 29-33 | IBM's four criteria as a scoring sheet. Cloud vs on-prem: default cloud, sovereignty exceptions. |
| 33-40 | Part 3 buying ladder SVG, then the ten questions - read 3-4 aloud, point to the rest. Red flags: expiring credits, "unlimited" fine print. |
| 40-42 | Pilot playbook: two engines, 2-4 weeks, your data, exit doors in writing. |
| 42-45 | Quiz + Q&A. Homework: get last quarter's bill (sets up a5). |

## Preflight

- Load the page, click both SVGs to test zoom, expand all cards once.
- Check projector legibility on the engine table - it is the densest element.
- Have a printable copy of the ten questions ready (execs ask for it).

## Never cut

- The amber line: most mid-size companies need a warehouse first.
- Pricing SHAPE per engine (the meter decides which habits get expensive).
- Expiring-credits red flag + the credits-that-vanished story.
- Pilot before commit: your data, your queries, your BI tool.

## Cut if long

- The DuckDB/Postgres self-study card (point to it, move on).
- Cloud vs on-prem history - keep only the 2026 default + sovereignty exception.
- Questions 6, 9, 10 of the ten (keep egress, paused cost, expiry, region).

## Likely exec questions

- "So which engine should WE buy?" - "The four criteria scored against your workload, then a two-engine pilot. Anyone who answers faster is selling."
- "Is the lakehouse just hype?" - "No - real tech, real fit at scale with ML. It is a stage-two purchase, not a first warehouse."
- "Why not stay on Postgres forever?" - "Fine until ~100 GB and single-node limits. The free row exists to calibrate, not to be the destination."
- "Are these prices current?" - "Shapes are stable, numbers move fast. Re-verify the week you buy - that is in the coverage note."
